About Fakhri Yarn

Woven into Dahod’s everyday life.

We’re a family business in Dahod, making rope and niwar since 2004. From our factory in Mega GIDC and our shop in Kathiriya Bazaar, we make the everyday things that quietly hold life together — cots and cattle, tents and tarpaulins, farms and festivals.

Buy a single bundle at the counter or a truckload on schedule — either way, you’re dealing with the people who actually make it. That’s how we keep the quality honest and the price fair.

🤝

One counter, two ways to buy

Households get a fair shop price. Traders and bulk buyers get factory-direct rates. Same material, same care.

📦

Depth of stock, always

Cotton, nylon, jute or plastic — in the thickness, colour and length you actually need. Most of it is already cut and waiting.

🚚

Packed and dispatched on time

We weigh, bundle and load your order at our GIDC factory ourselves, so it reaches you when we said it would.
